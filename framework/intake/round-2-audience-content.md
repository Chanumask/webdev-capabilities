# Round 2: Audience and content

Goal: know **who** visits, **what** they need to find and **where the content comes from**. Adapt to round 1: a listings site asks about item data, a landing page skips "pages".

## Q2.1 The visitors
Ask: Who are the most important visitors, and what do they want when they arrive?
Why: copy, hierarchy, tone, accessibility needs.
Type: multi, plus free text for the situation
Options (adapt to the project; examples):
- Private customers looking for something
- Business customers or partners
- Existing customers who need help
- Job seekers or applicants
Follow-up: for each chosen audience ask one short free-text question: "What is their situation and what do they want to do?" Ask the device split if unknown: mostly phone, mostly desktop, or both.
Writes: BRIEF.audience[]

## Q2.2 Tone of voice
Ask: How should the website sound?
Why: all copy and microcopy.
Type: single
Options:
- Calm and professional: clear, trustworthy, no hype (Recommended for services and finance)
- Warm and personal: friendly, direct address
- Premium and understated: few words, high confidence
- Bold and playful: energetic, personality first
Writes: BRIEF.voice
Follow-up: for German ask once whether to use "Sie" or "du".

## Q2.3 One page or several
Ask: How should the site be organised?
Why: routing, navigation, export format, CMS model.
Type: single
Options:
- One long page with sections (Recommended for prototypes and campaigns; simple and easy to send as a single file)
- A few pages (Home, Services, About, Contact)
- Many pages (blog, many items, sub-sections)
Writes: BRIEF.structure.kind

## Q2.4 Pages and sections
Ask: Which parts must the website have?
Why: sitemap and section list.
Type: multi. Tailor to the type; show the four most relevant and offer more on a second pass.
Options (examples):
- About us and team
- Services or products
- Listings, projects or references
- Contact form and details
Second pass candidates: FAQ, prices, blog or news, downloads, testimonials (only if real ones exist), partners, careers, map and opening hours, legal pages.
Writes: BRIEF.structure.sections[]
Always add Impressum and Datenschutz for German sites and mark them "placeholder until real data".

## Q2.5 Where do the words come from
Ask: Who provides the text?
Why: copywriting effort and placeholder policy.
Type: single
Options:
- Draft it for me: write good copy from this briefing; I review it (Recommended; marked as draft in the brief)
- I will provide it: texts will be pasted or sent
- Take it from the current website: reuse and improve
- Placeholder only: meaningful but clearly marked
Writes: BRIEF.content.copySource

## Q2.6 Images and media
Ask: What should the visuals be made of?
Why: asset pipeline, licensing and performance.
Type: multi
Options:
- 3D scenes and animation built in code: no image files needed, high impact (Recommended when the user wants an extraordinary or animated result)
- My own photos or videos: supplied by the user (ask when they will arrive)
- Illustrations or icons drawn for the project
- Stock photography: needs licensing; we only suggest sources and never download unlicensed images
Writes: BRIEF.media

## Q2.7 What changes over time
Ask: Which content will be updated regularly after launch, and by whom?
Why: decides whether a CMS is needed and what the editor model looks like (round 4).
Type: multi
Options:
- Nothing or rarely: texts stay as they are, content lives in files
- Items such as listings, projects or team: updated by non-technical staff
- News or blog posts: new entries regularly
- Prices, opening hours, events: small facts that change
Follow-up: ask "by whom" and "how often" as free text, and the editor's technical level (never technical, somewhat, developer).
Writes: BRIEF.content.dynamic[], BRIEF.content.editors

## Q2.8 Legal and privacy (Germany and EU)
Ask: Which of these apply?
Why: cookie banner, imprint, forms, analytics choices.
Type: multi
Options:
- Contact or enquiry forms collect personal data
- We want visitor statistics
- We embed external content (maps, videos, third-party fonts)
- None of these
Writes: BRIEF.legal
Agent note: default to no third-party requests (self-hosted fonts, no tracking). That avoids cookie banners.
