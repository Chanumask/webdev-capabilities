## Rules
- Every account is owned by the website owner (company or person), with a shared role address as login where possible.
- Helpers (developer, agency, agent) get their own named access, never the owner's login, and are removed when the work ends.
- Two-factor login on every account; recovery codes in the password manager and on paper in a safe place.
- Domain: auto-renew on, transfer lock on, contact email monitored.
