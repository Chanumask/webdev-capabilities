You do not need technical knowledge for anything in this guide.

Address: https://__CANONICAL_HOST__

## Changing texts, items and prices
1. Open the content system: ______ (address) and sign in with the account in `ACCOUNTS-AND-ACCESS.md`.
2. Open the collection you want (for example "Listings"), change the entry, press **Publish** (or Save, depending on the system).
3. The website updates by itself within a few minutes. If it does not, see "Nothing changed" below.

What you can change: texts, images in the entries, prices, items. What you should not change: layout, colours, animation. Those are part of the design and are changed by a developer.

## If your site has no content system
Send the change to ______ (person or agency) in writing: what, where, and the new text. They apply it and republish.

## Good habits
- Use clear images (about 1600 px wide, under 500 KB). Add a short description for each image (for people using screen readers).
- Check changes on a phone, because most visitors use one.
- Keep phone numbers, address and opening hours up to date in the Impressum and the contact section.
- Never share passwords in email or chat. Use the password manager.
