# Surface brief: Lindenhof one-page site (v2, restrained realism)

Scope: single route `/`, Persuade mode. Fictional property management company "Lindenhof Hausverwaltung" (German copy). Visitors: owners wanting management, tenants needing service; listings (rent/buy) are the content backbone.
Action: hero search (Mieten/Kaufen, Ort, Zimmer) filters the listing rows; tenant damage report; owner enquiry.
Memorable moment: scrolling plays construction backwards then forwards. The finished house is first; scroll rewinds to the empty building site; an excavator and tipper dig the pit, a mixer pours the slab, a tower crane raises the shell inside scaffolding, facade and interior are fitted floor by floor, the crane leaves, the garden arrives, and the camera pulls back into a neighbourhood.
Pinned by the user (2026-10-03): backgrounds must be less colourful and read as high quality; houses must look realistic with more detail; the floor-explosion animation is replaced by the construction story above. Client wants Wix Headless as CMS later.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A property company that shows how a home is made and then stays with it. The page is one continuous architectural film driven by scroll, refusing the stock-photo-hero-plus-card-grid real-estate portal.
OWN-WORLD: Architectural visualisation at dusk. Blue-black graphite ground, one warm brass accent, stone-grey light sections. Real materials: plaster, oak parquet, timber cladding, dark aluminium frames, glass, concrete, roof membrane. Sun-lit with soft shadows and ACES tone mapping. Hanken Grotesk light for display, regular for text. Engraved name plates (hairline, inset) for UI. No saturated colour fields.
STORY: Visitor sees a finished home, watches it being built by recognisable machines, understands that Lindenhof sells, rents and manages homes from first plan to daily service, then acts through search, damage report or enquiry.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Graphite field with a left scrim. Left: headline "Wohnen, das gut verwaltet ist." in light-weight Hanken Grotesk, short intro, and a chalk search plate (Mieten/Kaufen, Ort, Zimmer, "Objekte zeigen"). Right: the finished four-storey building with timber stair tower, balconies, rooftop sign and garden, seen from a low three-quarter angle. Name-plate nav top.
FORM: Restrained architectural-visualisation film (brief pinned by user, no roll), supersedes seed key c89ea79a Neues Frankfurt direction.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
